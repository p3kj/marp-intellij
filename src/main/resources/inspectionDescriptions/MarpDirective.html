Reports directive comments in a Marp deck that Marp ignores or misreads: a directive name that does not exist, a global directive written with an underscore, and an invalid value for paginate, math or headingDivider.

Marp drops such a directive without a hint, and the slide just looks wrong.

<!-- _theme: gaia -->
<!-- _clas: lead -->
<!-- paginate: yes -->

Unknown theme names are not reported here, the preview page shows a warning for them.
